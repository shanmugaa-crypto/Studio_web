About Us

26 Years of Professional Photography Excellence

Since 1998, Sri Mahalakshmi Studio has been the trusted name for professional photography in Nerkuppai and beyond. We specialize in capturing the authentic beauty of weddings, family celebrations, corporate events, and life's most cherished milestones with technical precision and artistic sensitivity.

Over 1500 events captured and countless families trust us with their memories. Our commitment is simple: to preserve your precious moments with the highest quality, ensuring that every photograph tells your unique story and stands the test of time.

With state-of-the-art equipment and decades of experience, we deliver professional-grade photography that goes beyond just images—we create treasured heirlooms that families will cherish for generations to come.
